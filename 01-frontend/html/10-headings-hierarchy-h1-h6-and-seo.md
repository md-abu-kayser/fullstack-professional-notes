# Headings Hierarchy (h1-h6) and SEO

Headings from h1 to h6 create a nested outline of a page's content, similar to a table of contents. Search engines weigh h1 heavily for topic relevance, and screen reader users often navigate a page heading by heading. Best practice is one h1 per page, followed by logically nested h2s and h3s without skipping levels.

```html
<h1>Page Title</h1>
<h2>Section</h2>
<h3>Subsection</h3>
```

**Key takeaway:** Skipping heading levels (h1 straight to h4) breaks the outline for assistive technology.
