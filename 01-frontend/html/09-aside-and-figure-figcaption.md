# aside and figure-figcaption

aside marks content tangentially related to the surrounding content, like a sidebar, pull quote, or ad. figure groups self-contained media (an image, diagram, or code snippet) with figcaption providing its caption, keeping the two semantically linked.

```html
<figure>
  <img src="chart.png" alt="Sales chart">
  <figcaption>Fig 1. Q3 sales by region</figcaption>
</figure>
```

**Key takeaway:** figcaption can appear before or after the media it describes - order does not matter semantically.
